DocuDeck Support

We are here to help you get the most out of DocuDeck on your iPhone and iPad.

How does DocuDeck handle my document privacy?

DocuDeck is built with zero-server on-device processing. All file edits (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, Text) and OCR scanning occur 100% on your device. We do not store or transmit your documents.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open DocuDeck, go to Settings > Restore Purchases. Apple will securely verify your Apple ID purchases and reactivate your subscription or lifetime access.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions can be managed directly on your device: Open iOS Settings > tap your Apple ID name at the top > tap Subscriptions > select DocuDeck > tap Cancel Subscription.

Which file formats can I open and edit?

DocuDeck natively supports viewing and editing Microsoft Word (.docx, .doc), Excel (.xlsx, .xls, .csv), PowerPoint (.pptx, .ppt), plain text (.txt, .md), RTF, and PDF documents.
